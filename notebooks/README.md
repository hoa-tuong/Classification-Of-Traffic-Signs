# Huong dan su dung Notebooks

Thu muc nay chua cac Jupyter Notebook phuc vu tung giai doan cua do an:

1. **`01_exploratory_data_analysis.ipynb`**:
   - Thong ke so luong anh theo tung lop bien bao (kiem tra co bi Imbalance khong).
   - Ve bieu do histogram ve kich thuoc anh, do sang trung binh.
   - Hien thi mot so mau anh ngau nhien cho tung class.

2. **`02_preprocessing_augmentation.ipynb`**:
   - Thu nghiem cac ky thuat tang cuong du lieu (Rotation, Shift, Color Jitter, Blur).
   - Can bang du lieu neu co nhan it anh hon han nhan khac.

3. **`03_baseline_model.ipynb`**:
   - Xay dung model CNN co ban hoac SVM + HOG lam baseline so sanh.

4. **`04_transfer_learning.ipynb`**:
   - Thu nghiem cac mo hinh SOTA: MobileNetV3, ResNet18/50, EfficientNet.
   - So sanh toc do suy luan (FPS) va do chinh xac.

5. **`05_model_evaluation.ipynb`**:
   - Tinh toan Confusion Matrix, Precision, Recall, Macro F1-score.
   - Phan tich cac truong hop mo hinh doan sai va giai thich ly do (anh mo, khuat, nguoc sang).
